NAME: Pulse-Floater Cultivation Pod
NICKNAME: "The Singing Pod"

SPECS:
- Diameter: approximately 18 cm (human-head scale)
- Mass: 1.2 kg when fully gel-charged
- Pulse rate: 0.8–1.2 Hz, modulated by ambient algae diurnal cycle
- Buoyancy: neutral-to-positive in EZ-gel brine, drifts with 0.3 N net lift
- Internal volume: ~1.4 L translucent membrane cavity
- Biotope core: 40 g processed Cladosporium sphaerospermum pellet
- Operating temperature: 14–22 °C (pool-coupled)
- Operational lifespan: 14–18 months before membrane fatigue and reseeding

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Spherical translucent membrane balloon with a faint hexagonal sheen where light refracts through the EZ-gel
- Soft amber-amber glow emanating from the core pellet, waxing and waning on a slow rhythm
- Visible swirl of algae-green suspended particulate drifting inside the gel
- Surface texture soft and yielding, dimpling slightly under pole pressure
- Outer skin occasionally beaded with condensation from humid The Pearl atmosphere
- A pale, vein-like reticulation marks the membrane where the Aegis-strain mycelial threads anchor internally

MATERIALS:
- living_mycelium_composite_aegis (Aegis-Strain Fungus Membrane Shell)
- ez_water_gel_structured (EZ-Water (Structured Hexagonal Phase Gel))
- cladosporium_biomass_pellet (Cladosporium sphaerospermum Radiosynthesis Pellet)
- peek_victrex (PEEK Polymer Reinforcement Ring at Pole-Contact Pole)
- fused_silica (Fused Silica Optical Window for Glow Transmission)
- algae_suspension_chlorella (Chlorella Algae Suspension, Working Fluid)
- alumina_995 (Alumina Ceramic Core Scaffold for Biomass Pellet)
- ionic_liquid_emim (Ionic Liquid Nutrient Carrier in Gel Matrix)

FUNCTION:
- Drifts through EZ Aqua Core aquaponics pools, seeding and oxygenating water columns
- Converts trace background radiation into usable biochemical energy via radiosynthesis
- Regulates local CO₂ and dissolved oxygen through algal symbiosis
- Provides gentle bioluminescent illumination for cultivator night-shifts
- Serves as a living sentinel — its pulse indicates pool pH, nutrient flow, and water health
- Reads and responds to circadian cues from surrounding Chlorella cultures
- Acts as a slow-release fertilizer and structured-water seeder when eventually harvested

HISTORY:
- Developed during the early colonization era when Oris first stabilized the Cathedral and began flash-purifying water into EZ-gel in The Crypt
- Early prototypes were rigid polymer spheres that failed under osmotic pressure; cultivator Maja Vossen proposed using living Aegis-strain membrane after observing its self-sealing behavior in Root Weave
- Became standard aquaponics equipment by Year 340 After Launch, replacing mechanical aerators that fouled the pools
- Mira Solheim began tending pods in Year 808 After Launch, after her sister Lin's recovery drew her from maintenance at Root Weave Junction 17-Alpha
- Known colloquially among Third-Tier cultivators as "the choir" for their layered, asynchronous pulsing across a drift
- The phrase "the vats do not sing" entered EZ Aqua Core oral tradition after Solheim's logbook entry of Shift Fourteen, Year 812
