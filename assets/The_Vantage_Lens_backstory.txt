In the lower industrial decks where Oris reigns, maintenance workers speak in hushed tones about the silent shapes that drift through service corridors at the third shift change. The Vantage Lens is an autonomous sensor platform grown from the same Aegis-fungus that lines Ombra Prime's hull, cultivated for a deeply unsettling purpose: to watch.

It is a levitating ovoid the size of a clenched fist, its body organic black with a wet membrane-like sheen. At its center sits a single unblinking eye of crystallized EZ-water gel, ringed by fine filaments of conductive polymer that pulse with faint amber bioluminescence. These filaments draw just enough current from nearby Hydro-Capacitor nodes to glide silently through the air, without motors, without sound, without the comfortable hum of machinery that gives a person warning they are being observed. It simply is there, recording thermal signatures and transmitting encrypted telemetry to Oris's Cathedral in The Gut.

The logs are kept by Halvar Yngvason, a veteran maintenance supervisor in the upper Root Weave who has spent thirty-one years threading the dark passages between The City and the lower industrial sectors. He carries three personal Vantage Lenses that have bonded to his bioelectric signature, granted by Oris as a mark of trust he has never quite decided whether to accept with pride or dread. They hover at his shoulders like faithful hounds, and he has grown accustomed to their presence the way one grows accustomed to a scar.
