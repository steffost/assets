NAME: Gloom Binder
NICKNAME: "The Sleeve"

SPECS:
- Overall length: 142 mm
- Diameter: 58 mm
- Mass: 1.4 kg (loaded)
- Trigger actuation force: 2.2 N
- Reservoir capacity: 60 mL structured EZ-water
- Disc chamber internal volume: 22 mL
- Operating temperature range: -40°C to +85°C
- Acoustic signature: dormant <8 dB, digesting ~22 dB, fresh-feed whine ~51 dB
- Safety: dead-man's cut-off releases pressure if grip is lost for >0.4 s

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Squat, palm-sized cylinder resembling a fat finger-sized canister with a flattened top cap
- Dull gunmetal-gray core showing through hundreds of cargo-bay scuffs, dents, and paint transfers
- Woven sleeve in muted bone-and-olive tones, its mycelial weave visibly tightening or relaxing around the cylinder in response to the disc's state
- Small vent slots near the top ringed in copper-colored alloy, faintly warm to the touch
- Trigger bar set in a recessed channel along the lower third, with a knurled grip
- Hairline seam running vertically where the sleeve fastens with a pair of old brass press-studs
- Faint pulse of amber light through the weave when the disc is digesting

FUNCTION:
- Detects and contains raw Vitalis spills in open deck or cargo environments
- Sedates and stabilizes wild Vitalis into a harvestable paste without destroying its energetic content
- Provides acoustic early-warning of distant or recent spills via disc resonance
- Prevents Ghosting symptoms in nearby machinery and personnel
- Feeds a contained disc of Vitalis residue that acts as both attractor and capture medium

HISTORY:
- Developed during the deep cycles of the Industrial Domain after Skimmer-bots on the Aegis hull began exhibiting Ghosting symptoms
- Commissioned by Oris to act as a scalpel in place of the previous hammer-and-destroy containment approach
- First prototype nearly killed its inventor when the disc absorbed a Chaos bleed and emitted a frequency that rearranged the bones in his hand
- Schumann-resonance dampener and dead-man's cut-off added to all current production models after the prototype incident
- Standard issue on every HazTech belt since the Root Weave adoption cycle
- Referenced in Marta Brun's logbook, Year 847 After Launch, during a quiet containment at Junction 9-Charlie
