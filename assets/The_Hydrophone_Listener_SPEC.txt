NAME: Hydrophone Listener
NICKNAME: "The Ear"

SPECS:
- Dimensions: 110 mm × 65 mm × 28 mm (palm-sized, single-hand operable)
- Weight: 184 grams (dry), 207 grams (gel-reservoir fully charged)
- Acoustic sensitivity: -38 dB re 1 μPa, frequency range 5 Hz – 42 kHz
- Forward aperture diameter: 22 mm with acoustic impedance-matched waveguide
- Piezoelectric array: 16-element cross-configured PZT cluster
- EZ-gel reservoir capacity: 14 mL, refill cycle approximately 90 days under standard use
- Indicator: single amber bioluminescent emitter, pulse rate 0.5–4 Hz tied to anomaly amplitude
- Docking port: recessed Nexus Symbioticus interface, 7-pin wet-contact
- Operational temperature: -10°C to +65°C (tolerates tunnel condensate)
- Power: parasitic draw from docked Nexus, no internal cell

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Elongated lozenge form, slightly tapered toward the forward aperture
- Outer shell visibly layered — concentric growth rings of pale grey-beige fungal material with darker striations between layers
- Six brushed titanium ribs arc lengthwise along the housing like finger bones, slightly proud of the shell surface, showing minor wear and oxidation patina
- Forward aperture ringed in matte titanium, interior chamber faintly visible as a dark hexagonal well
- Amber indicator set in a teardrop-shaped window on the upper face, glowing softly when active
- Nexus port recessed into the lower rear edge, surrounded by a soft biological gasket
- Overall impression: half-tool, half-organ; tactile, warm to the touch, slightly damp
- Surface microtexture of mycelium gives subtle suede-like grip

MATERIALS:
- mycelium_composite_living (Aegis-fungus layered shell, outer housing)
- titanium_grade5 (Brushed titanium reinforcement ribs and aperture ring)
- piezoelectric_pzt (PZT piezoelectric crystal arrays, acoustic sensing chamber)
- ionic_liquid_emim (EZ-gel stabilized acoustic coupling medium in internal reservoir)
- peek_victrex (PEEK internal structural frame and Nexus port insulator)
- fused_silica (Fused silica optical waveguide channeling amber indicator light)
- kevlar_49 (Kevlar 49 tether loop and lanyard anchor reinforcement)
- aluminum_7075 (Aluminum 7075 internal resonance chamber backplate)

FUNCTION:
- Detects sub-audible and ultrasonic anomalies in deep infrastructure (pipe cracks, seal failures, microbial blooms, pressure anomalies)
- Translates acoustic signatures into neural-interpretable signals via docked Nexus Symbioticus
- Provides ambient pulse-rate feedback for operators without neural link
- Pinpoints directional source of detected anomalies for maintenance routing
- Refillable EZ-gel reservoir extends sensitivity floor below conventional microphone limits
- Survives humid, corrosive tunnel environments that destroy standard electronics

HISTORY:
- Cultivated rather than manufactured in fungal-growth vats within The Root Weave's lower biomanufactury levels
- Outer shells grown over titanium armature skeletons harvested from decommissioned hull plating of early-generation orbital infrastructure
- Piezoelectric crystal arrays salvaged from recycled sonar equipment originally used in abyssal depth surveying
- Adopted as standard issue for the Acoustic Survey Corps approximately forty years ago, replacing earlier purely mechanical stethoscope-probes
- Marek Solenne's unit is an estimated 15–20 years old, recovered from a Sector Five storage locker and revived through personal maintenance
- The amber indicator's slower pulse in Solenne's unit is a known sign of partial mycelium senescence, not failure — veterans trust these aged instruments over fresh cultivates
