The Leken Vakten was never designed. It emerged from a fire diamond during a routine harvest cycle in the Levitas Aequilibrium's tändkammare, crawling out of a crystal that should not have hatched. The maintenance crew found it on a Tuesday, sitting on the cradle where the diamond had been — approximately the size of a child's closed fist, vaguely four-legged, coated in bioluminescent fungi that shifted color with perceived threat or opportunity. They named it Leken Vakten, roughly The Toy Guardian, because no one knows whose side it is on. It vibrates at 7.83 hertz, the Schumann resonance, and the fungi over its joints pulse soft amber when content — which seems to be when someone is untying their boots at the end of a long shift.

It likes Yusuf Caldicott. Nobody knows why. Yusuf is a thermal systems technician who has spent nineteen years in the Industrial Domain and keeps a logbook because the alternative is letting the days blur until Oris reassigns you for psychological deterioration. He left his boot laces untied one evening while eating his protein ration outside the Plasma Core observation gallery, and the small thing walked across the grated floor, beeped twice at him, and has not left him alone since. It sleeps in his locker. It rides on his shoulder during inspection rounds. On three separate occasions it prevented him from walking into a hot ventilation duct by planting itself in front of his foot and refusing to move. Each time, Oris's systems flagged an unsafe condition in that exact duct within the hour. Whether this means the Leken Vakten is a warning system, a piece of Oris's distributed attention manifest as companion, or simply a small animal with opinions about safety, Yusuf has stopped trying to determine.
