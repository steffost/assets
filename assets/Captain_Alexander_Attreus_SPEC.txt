NAME: Echo Stone (Memory Crystal)
NICKNAME: "The Echo"

SPECS:
- Mass: 187 grams
- Dimensions: 78 mm × 54 mm × 41 mm (irregular, palm-conformant)
- Internal core volume: 14.2 cm³ of active biological matrix
- Acoustic pickup range: 32 Hz – 14 kHz
- Radiotrophic metabolism threshold: 0.3 μSv/h ambient ionizing radiation minimum
- Memory encoding latency: 0.8 seconds audio-to-lattice integration
- Thermal output: +2.4°C above ambient during active recall sessions
- Lattice growth rate: ~0.7 mm per lunar cycle under IR tuning at 850 nm

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Asymmetrical teardrop silhouette with one flattened facet where it rests against the temple
- Outer fused silica shell carries a milky, cloud-like translucence with internal hairline fractures that catch plotting-table lamplight as faint amber threads
- Suspended within the gel, the Cladosporium colony forms a deep umber-to-violet nebula, denser at the stone's center, thinning toward the edges
- Faint dendritic vein patterns visible along the surface where decades of mycelial growth have pressed against the silica boundary
- Surface is cool and slightly tacky to the touch; warms perceptibly during recall
- A single shallow concavity on the underside fits the curve of the thenar eminence

MATERIALS:
- fused_silica (Fused Silica (SiO₂) — outer growth matrix shell)
- ionic_liquid_emim (Ionic Liquid [EMIM][TF2N] — stabilized EZ-gel suspension medium)
- biological_material: Cladosporium sphaerospermum (Aegis strain), melanin-enriched radiotrophic mycelium (living internal substrate)
- sapphire_synthetic (Synthetic Sapphire (Al₂O₃) — microlens inlays at acoustic pickup nodes)
- peek_victrex (PEEK — internal acoustic waveguide filaments)
- perovskite_solar (Perovskite Solar Film — thin IR harvest layer beneath the upper facet)
- piezoelectric_pzt (PZT Piezoelectric Ceramic — vibration pickup element at the temple contact point)

FUNCTION:
- Records and replays audio memories encoded as mycelial growth-density variations
- Metabolizes ambient ionizing radiation into biological growth energy, rendering the unit self-sustaining aboard Ombra Prime
- Tuned IR exposure pathway (850 nm) biases lattice growth into stable, retrievable patterns
- Serves as a personal mnemonic anchor for the Captain — no data retrieval interface, only direct skin contact and focused listening
- Acoustic waveguide converts whispered input into pressure waves that the gel matrix conducts to the living core

HISTORY:
- Acquired by Captain Attreus in Metropolis during his second year as a junior navigation officer, from a vendor of the Adaptant faction of Vitae Cultus
- Purchase price: a promise to remember
- Does not appear on any ship manifest; Executive Officer Oris is aware and has chosen not to log it
- Has accompanied Attreus through every major command decision of his career, including the Year 891 gas giant course-correction debate
- Used most often on Third Watch, when the weight of a hundred thousand sleeping souls is loudest on the Forward Command Bridge
