The Wisp-Latch is a palm-sized collar clip worn throughout Ombra Prime, reading the wearer's bioelectrical signature through micro-needles in the outer garment and translating stress, temperature, and neural fatigue into a soft colored pulse: green for steady, amber for slowing, red for unsafe. Above the pulse sits a hollow nautilus-shaped resonance chamber harvested from the same Aegis-fungus cultures lining the hull, which gives the device its name by chiming softly when the ship's stress fractures groan or the Cathedral shifts its computational load.

LOGBOOK ENTRY:

Personal log, Seventeen, sanitation tech, fourth rotation.

Lower Gut, between the Spine and the Cathedral intake.

Year 891 After Launch.

I almost did not clip it on this morning. The uniform inspector at Junction Nine gave me a look when my Latch was hanging loose instead of seated. I told her the clasp has been stiff since the humidity shift two weeks ago, which is true but not the reason. The real reason is that Aldis on nights wears his the same way and last week his glowed steady green for an entire shift while mine burned amber by the second hour, and I did not like what that said about me in front of the Cathedral intake crew. So I stopped wearing it. Then this morning I watched a man in the Spine junction collapse from exhaustion nobody had seen coming, because his Latch had been tucked inside his coverall where the light could not be read, and I went home and dug mine out of the drawer and clipped it on properly. The clasp is still stiff.

Sometimes in the deep tunnels, when the dark has weight, the Latch whispers without reason, a single soft note like someone breathing very far away.

NAME: Wisp-Latch
