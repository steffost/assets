NAME: The Verdant Spire
NICKNAME: "The Singing Tower"

SPECS:
- Height: 3 meters
- Tapered cylindrical form resembling a cathedral organ pipe
- Hexagonal basalt base, approximately 60 cm in diameter
- Three internal rotating tiers operating on independent Vitalis frequency bands
- Thousands of hair-thin internal capillary channels for EZ-water distribution
- Single brass observation port mounted near the base
- One embedded stabilized Vitalis plasma seed-crystal per unit
- Magnetic-field-driven tier rotation system

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Outer shell of pearl-white bioceramic interwoven with pale, faintly luminous Aegis-fungus mycelium
- Translucent body panels allowing internal tier illumination to bleed outward
- Lowest tier glows deep green (Terra frequency)
- Middle tier glows soft emerald (Flora frequency)
- Upper tier pulses silver-white (Nexus frequency)
- Dark volcanic basalt base with visible mineral grain
- Brass observation lens with hand-ground magnification, surrounded by an etched ring of glyphs
- Surface exhibits subtle capillary breathing patterns when active
- Overall silhouette tapers from wider base to narrow apex

MATERIALS:
- Aegis-fungus mycelium (cultivated living outer layer)
- Pearl-white bioceramic composite (structural shell)
- Dark volcanic basalt (hexagonal base)
- Stabilized Vitalis plasma seed-crystal (embedded in base)
- Brass (observation port housing and lens)
- Crystalline growth platforms (internal tiers)
- EZ-water (circulating fluid medium)

FUNCTION:
- Vertical multi-tier cultivation of plants, mosses, lichens, and stasis-sensitive specimens
- Mineral-heavy root cultivation on the Terra tier
- Engineered moss and lichen photosynthesis on the Flora tier
- Meditational seed and stasis dreaming cultivation on the Nexus tier
- Bioacoustic communication channel between Warden and structure
- Alleged scrying interface for glimpsing the next target planet through the seed-crystal

HISTORY:
- Descended from the first prototype cultivation towers brought aboard the colony vessel at Launch
- Adopted as the signature instrument of the Pearl Wardens, cloistered botanist-monks of The Pearl's inner sanctum
- Each Warden is assigned a personal Spire at age seven during apprenticeship, with the bond intended to last a lifetime
- Upon a Warden's death, their Spire is traditionally buried in the basalt gardens beneath the central waterfall
- Some buried Spires reportedly continue emitting harmonic tones for weeks before the seed-crystal fully dims
- The oldest surviving Spires carry inscriptions in the old colonists' shorthand — warnings, dedications, and prayers
- Rumors persist among apprentices that the oldest Spires can reveal visions of uncharted worlds when pressed at the observation port during twilight hours
