NAME: The Lustral Pendant
NICKNAME: "The Pearl's Tear"

SPECS:
- Overall dimensions: Approximately 45 mm in length, 28 mm in width, 12 mm in thickness (teardrop profile, flattened)
- Mass: 14–18 grams depending on gel volume
- Core reservoir volume: 0.6 ml stabilized amber gel
- Surface glaze thickness: 0.3 mm living biofilm layer
- Operational thermal range: 18°C to 41°C (glow response band)
- Service life: Indefinite; biofilm glaze requires rehydration every 3–4 years
- Hanging aperture: 4 mm reinforced cord channel for waxed fiber cord

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Flattened teardrop silhouette, roughly the size of a human thumb
- Matte ceramic body in pale ash-grey with subtle warm undertones
- Iridescent bands shifting across the surface in humid light — faint rose, green, and indigo bloom from the biofilm glaze
- Central raised setting of brushed ceramic cradling a translucent amber droplet
- Amber core ranges in hue from fossilized honey (warm, near body) to old tea (cool, dormant)
- Faint inner luminescence when warmed by skin contact; reads as a soft candle-glow
- Edge micro-chamfer of 0.5 mm; slight asymmetry confirming hand-pressing
- Single small condensate bead may form on the surface after prolonged darkness

MATERIALS:
- fungus_farm_ash_ceramic (Kiln-fired ash harvested from fungal substrate, hand-pressed)
- living_biofilm_glaze (Living biofilm derived from EZ-aqua basin lining)
- stabilized_amber_gel_concentrate (Decades-tinctured EZ-water concentrate with dissolved mineral salts)
- brushed_ceramic_setting (Low-fired silicate ceramic for the central bezel)
- vespel_sp1 (Vespel SP-1 polyimide thread-reinforced cord channel liner)
- fused_silica_microbead (Fused silica micro-inclusion suspended in the gel as a thermal luminescence seed)
- ionic_liquid_emim (Ionic liquid [EMIM][TF2N] trace fraction within the gel, lowering viscosity and extending luminescence persistence)

FUNCTION:
- Ceremonial insignia of senior care authority within the Pearl's living systems
- Passive temperature-reactive luminary; glow intensity tracks wearer pulse warmth
- Humidity-responsive surface that develops condensate micro-droplets under prolonged low-light conditions
- Symbolic bond-object linking caretaker to the EZ-water reservoirs they tend
- Tradition marker; only gifted to tenders with fifteen or more years of service

HISTORY:
- Tradition originated in the third generation of the voyage, founded by senior cultivator Breta Sallow
- Originally fashioned from residue of tending work so the maker's "attention" would persist in the gardens
- Never sold or traded; exclusively gifted across generations of tenders
- Six pendants gifted by Breta Sallow over her lifetime; her personal pendant worn continuously for sixty-three years
- Currently held in trust for succession — to be transferred to Yenna, next keeper of the upper terraces, Year 891 After Launch
