NAME: Occulted Mirrors of Sector 7
NICKNAME: "The Quiet"

SPECS:
- Vertical surface dimensions: approximately 1.8 meters tall by 0.9 meters wide
- Activation range: triggers within 30 centimeters of a human face
- Composition: two-layer bonded structure (polished front alloy over biological capillary lattice)
- Response time: instantaneous darkening upon facial detection; amber pulse latency under one second
- Breath-tracking synchronization: confirmed within 0.5-second tolerance (Bramwell's empirical tests)
- Power source: passive, fed by continuous flow of Vitalis Plasma through the Aegis capillary bed
- No data transmission or reception capability (verified by shipboard scanners)

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Inactive state: jet-black polished obsidian-alloy surface, mirror-clear when no face is within range, reflecting surroundings with perfect fidelity
- Active state: alloy darkens to deep ink-black, becoming opaque and light-absorbing
- Underlying capillary bed emits soft amber glow when activated, pulsing in rhythm with the viewer's respiration
- Amber luminescence is specifically described as evoking hearth-light warmth, a firelit glow no crew member has ever experienced firsthand
- Framed in simple dark metal mountings, bolted directly to bulkheads
- Typically installed in service corridors of the Outer Ring maintenance levels, often near junctions, hydration stations, or sealed compartment doors

MATERIALS:
- Front surface: Polished Obsidian Alloy, a dense black metallic compound native to Pearl's industrial stocks
- Backing layer: Living lattice of Aegis Fungus Mycelium, cultivated over decades into dense capillary networks
- Circulating fluid: Vitalis Plasma, harvested from Sub-Orb migration currents, flowing in thin continuous streams through the mycelial bed
- Frame: Standard dark metal structural alloy, utilitarian mounting hardware

FUNCTION:
- Facial detection and recognition through unknown mechanism, despite lacking conventional sensor architecture
- Visual manifestation of apparent memories retained in the Vitalis Plasma's residue of every face ever reflected in the surface
- Rhythmic biofeedback response that synchronizes with the viewer's breathing pattern
- Capable of displaying specific faces not belonging to the viewer (Bramwell consistently sees a woman with his own features)
- No diagnostic, communicative, or utilitarian purpose recognized by the ship's official technical classification
- Functions as a passive contemplative surface during inactive periods, serving as ordinary reflective mirror

HISTORY:
- Emerged organically over decades within the perpetually humming service corridors of the Pearl's outer maintenance ring
- Neither designed by the Genetic Archivists nor sanctioned by the Orthodox clergy of Vitae Cultus
- Condemned as "Desecrations" by the Orthodox faction
- Dismissed as "Distractions" by the Reformed faction
- Third-shift maintenance technicians refer to them simply as "quiet"
- Scientific speculation attributes the phenomenon to a feedback loop between Aegis mycelium's natural biosensitivity and residual memory stored in Vitalis Plasma
- Notable documented interaction: Captain-Surgeon Eliott Bramwell (age 52), Year 891 After Launch, recorded a recurring vision of a woman wearing pre-Archivist mantle robes, bearing his own eyes and jaw, mouthing an unreadable syllable at Junction 7-Alpha
- The apparitions remain patient and persistent, awaiting new observers without urgency or apparent agenda
