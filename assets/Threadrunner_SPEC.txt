NAME: Threadrunner Personal Transit Apparatus
NICKNAME: "The Pulse"

SPECS:
- Six-legged articulated limb configuration with adhesive gripper tips
- Approximate chassis length: 2.1 meters (rider-facing profile)
- Operating envelope: vertical, inverted, and lateral surfaces (walls, ceilings, structural members)
- Haptic steering collar with biopotential muscle contraction detection
- Respiration-synced gait modulation (rider pulse integration)
- Maximum sustainable vertical travel: approximately 600 meters between rest points
- Ambient power draw from thermal differentials and trace atmospheric bioaerosol ingestion
- Rider interface: single contact point (neck haptic collar, no handlebars or saddle)

VISUAL DESIGN:
- Skeletal, open-lattice chassis resembling dried marine coral or sun-bleached driftwood
- Pale ivory to bone-cream coloration with faint blue-grey veining along stress lines
- Six multi-jointed limbs with exposed tendon-like cable bundles running along their length
- Adhesive gripper pads on each terminal segment, visibly textured with concentric organic rings
- No enclosed cockpit; rider is suspended in a slim cradle sling between the limb cluster
- Overall silhouette evokes a skeletal insect or arachnid exoskeleton, hollow rather than solid
- Subtle luminescence at limb joints during active gait cycles, warm amber in color

MATERIALS:
- mycelium_composite_living (Living Mycelium Composite — primary skeletal frame)
- kevlar_49 (Kevlar 49 Composite — internal tendon cables and load-bearing strands)
- peek_victrex (PEEK — haptic collar housing and neural contact interface)
- piezoelectric_pzt (PZT Piezoelectric Ceramic — pulse-sensing nodes in collar and joint actuators)
- nitinol (Nitinol — limb articulation springs and shape-recovery elements)
- fused_silica (Fused Silica — optical data conduit threading along dorsal spine)
- galinstan (Galinstan — micro-fluidic thermal regulation channels within chassis)
- mother_of_pearl (Mother of Pearl — outer gripper pad surface for traction and wear resistance)

FUNCTION:
- Personal transit through vertical and inverted urban surfaces where wheeled vehicles cannot operate
- Bypasses elevator congestion in high-density vertical city sectors
- Intuitive steering via neuromuscular intent rather than manual controls
- Gait rhythm adapts to rider's physiological state (heart rate, stress, exertion)
- Package and capsule transport for courier operations across extreme elevation differentials
- Modular cargo cradle accommodates sealed containers up to courier-cassette dimensions

HISTORY:
- Developed for the Metropolis vertical labyrinth, where street-level vehicles congest and elevators cannot scale fast enough
- Bio-grown chassis lineage traces to early mycelium structural experiments repurposed for locomotion
- Earned the affectionate nickname "The Pulse" among residents for its heartbeat-synchronized amber glow
- Unit 47-C of this class has been in continuous courier service for at least nine years under a single rider
- Trusted by independent couriers for high-stakes, time-critical deliveries across dangerous elevation gaps
- Represents a deliberate design philosophy of organic utility, as if the apparatus chose to remain useful after biological cessation
